aws s3 cp LOCAL s3://BUCKET/raw/DOCSET/VERSION/file.pdf

This path shape matters for event-driven pipelines: raw/<doc-set>/<version>/file.pdf
lets downstream jobs infer document set and version without parsing the PDF.

Before you sync thousands of pages to a Knowledge Base, estimate total tokens and
preview chunk boundaries locally. Re-ingesting while you are still tuning chunking
strategy can get expensive—measure first, sync once you trust the splits.
